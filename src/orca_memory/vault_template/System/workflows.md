# Workflows

These workflows are intentionally small and tentative. The Owner may revise
them in [`rules.md`](rules.md).

## Capture

1. Save a supplied article, document, clipping, or link in `Inbox/Raw/` when
   the Owner explicitly asks to retain it.
2. Save a quick thought, idea, observation, or reminder in `Inbox/Notes/` when
   the Owner explicitly asks to retain it.
3. Keep the original meaning, attribution, uncertainty, and source details.

## Review and curation

1. Read the selected intake and the destination that may receive it.
2. Separate supported facts, decisions, interpretations, and open questions.
3. Confirm the request authorizes the canonical move or update; ask only when
   that authority is missing.
4. Keep unresolved items in intake and retain provenance when archiving is
   explicitly authorized.

## Conversation memory

Conversation artifacts belong under `System/Orca Memory/` and remain
noncanonical. Saving or applying them is never automatic in this starter.
